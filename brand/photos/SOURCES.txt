The photographs the kit draws on, all released under CC0 1.0 (public domain
dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), kept here
resized to at most 5120 px wide and recompressed. The kit renders them in a
green duotone under the logo. The first two also come as wallpapers and
covers; the rest are surfaces of the generator.
