Clear space

Keep free space around the logo equal to the side of the mark's window, x, on every side.

Minimum size

Never smaller than 24 px tall for the mark, or 96 px wide for the lockup.

Don't

  • Recolor it

  • Stretch or squash it

  • Rotate it

  • Add shadows, glows or other effects

  • Outline it

  • Place it on a busy background

Loading

Seven animated versions of the mark for loading states, each in two files: the mark in its gradient for light surfaces, and in white for dark ones. Every file is a self-contained SVG with no script: it works inline, in an image tag and as a background. Show the still mark instead when someone prefers reduced motion.
