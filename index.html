About Apysyk

Approved descriptions. Quote them as they are.

One-liner

Apysyk is the operating layer where security teams see risk, decide priority, execute fixes, and prove the work is done.

Short boilerplate

Apysyk is not another scanner. It is where security teams collect findings, choose priorities, assign owners, execute fixes, and prove the work is done.

Long boilerplate

Apysyk is not another scanner. It is where security teams collect findings, choose priorities, assign owners, execute fixes, and prove the work is done. Software now moves faster than security can operate. Findings are everywhere, ownership is unclear, and the work gets lost between scanners, tickets, pipelines, and people. Apysyk OS turns findings into owned, fixed, and verified security work.

Clear space

Keep free space around the logo equal to the height of the cube's inner square, x, on every side.

Minimum size

Never smaller than 24 px tall for the mark, or 96 px wide for the lockup.

Don't

  • Recolor it

  • Stretch or squash it

  • Rotate it

  • Add shadows, glows or other effects

  • Outline it

  • Place it on a busy background
